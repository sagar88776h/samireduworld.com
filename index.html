About me

A teacher who listens first.

I teach History, Political Science, Geography and Economics to classes 9 to 12 at a government school, and Political Science at college. In ten years in the classroom I’ve learned that most students don’t struggle with the subject. They struggle with how it was explained to them.

So I teach history as the story of real people, politics through the news students already follow, and geography and economics through the world right outside their window. Samir Edu World is where I share my notes, free, with any student who needs them.
