About me

A teacher who listens first.

I teach History and English at a government school in Tripura. In twelve years I’ve learned that most students don’t struggle with the subject. They struggle with how it was explained to them.

So I teach history as the story of real people and real choices, and English as a language you use every day, not just for exams. Samir Edu World is where I share my notes, free, with any student who needs them.
