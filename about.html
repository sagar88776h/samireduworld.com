About me

A teacher who listens first.

I’m a tuition teacher for college students, and Political Science is my main subject. I also teach Education. In ten years of teaching I’ve learned that most students don’t struggle with the subject. They struggle with how it was explained to them.

So I teach politics through the news students already follow: every theory, every article of the Constitution, connected to something real. Samir Edu World is where students can find my notes and reach me for tuition.
